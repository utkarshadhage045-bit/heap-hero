Heap Rules

↓
Min Heap

Every parent is less than or equal to its children. The smallest value sits at the root.

↑
Max Heap

Every parent is greater than or equal to its children. The largest value sits at the root.

⌘
Array indexes

For a node at index i: left child = 2i + 1, right child = 2i + 2, parent = floor((i − 1) / 2).
